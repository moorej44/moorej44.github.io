Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This privacy policy applies to the On-Call Notification System ("System"), which uses SMS and voice messaging to notify designated on-call staff of incoming requests.

Information We Collect

We collect phone numbers of staff members who are designated as on-call recipients. No other personal information is collected or stored.

How We Use Information

Phone numbers are used solely to deliver on-call notifications via SMS and voice call. They are not used for marketing purposes.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose phone numbers or message content to any third parties, except as required to deliver messages through our telecommunications provider (Twilio).

Data Retention

Phone numbers are retained only as long as the staff member is part of the on-call rotation. Numbers are removed upon request or when no longer needed.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the system administrator.

Terms and Conditions

Last updated: March 2026

These terms apply to the On-Call Notification System, which delivers SMS and voice notifications to designated on-call healthcare staff.

Program Description

The On-Call Notification System sends SMS and voice alerts to staff members who are part of a rotating on-call schedule. Message frequency varies based on on-call activity, typically low volume.

Consent

Recipients are staff members who have been added to the on-call rotation by a system administrator as part of their role responsibilities.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.

Opt-Out

To opt out of notifications, reply STOP to any message. You will receive a confirmation and no further messages will be sent.

Help

Reply HELP to any message for support, or contact the system administrator directly.

Contact

For questions, contact the system administrator.
